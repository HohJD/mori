How it works

Three buttons.
That's the manual.

  • No camera. A real mute.A physical switch cuts the microphone. The little light matches what the hardware is actually doing.
  • Works offline.Pats, games, timers, and naps all run on the device. Wi-Fi is only for chatting.
  • Never dies of neglect.Busy week? Mori is just happy to see you. No streaks, no guilt, no sad pixels.
  • Your memories, your call.See, edit, or delete what Mori remembers. Cancel a plan and your buddy stays.

    AI connections ⌄

    “Ready to try” means configured, not yet verified. “Replied this visit” means this AI has answered here.

      The owner needs to enable AI access and add a provider on the server. There’s no key to paste here.

      Setting up your own Mori?

      Follow docs/AI_CONNECTIONS.md in the project. Set an owner access code, then explicitly enable an eligible Gateway route on your linked Vercel project or add a direct provider key. A configured route still needs a real request to verify it.

      Privacy & how it works ⌄

      Talk listens once, adds your words to the draft, then sends when you finish. No microphone starts on load. Your browser’s speech service may process audio off-device; read-aloud uses browser speech synthesis. This is turn-taking, not native realtime voice. Typing is always an alternative.

      Only the selected AI’s chat goes through Mori to that provider, via Vercel AI Gateway when enabled. Chats stay in page memory and aren’t saved by this app. Providers and Gateway may retain data under their policies. AI can make mistakes.

      Provider APIs have separate access and billing. ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini app subscriptions and history don’t transfer. No provider keys go in this page. Your owner code uses an HttpOnly session cookie, not browser storage. Stop cancels waiting here; a request may already have reached the provider.

      Your companion, focus tasks, wins, and theme stay in browser storage. New conversation clears only the selected chat. Mori has no email, calendar, or account tools. The device is a design concept, not a product for sale.
