    A few human questions

    Small friend.

    A little clarity before the attachment begins.

    Can I buy the little device?+

    Not yet. The hardware is an illustrated design concept, not a manufactured product. There’s nothing to buy or reserve here. The web companion is yours to explore now.

    Is Mori using AI or listening to me?+

    No. This Mori uses hand-written reactions and a local timer. There’s no conversational AI, microphone connection, or recording. Just an authored little character.

    What does Mori remember?+

    Your chosen name and shell, interaction progress, current focus session, and up to 24 recent wins are saved in this browser’s local storage. Task text never leaves your browser. There are no accounts, analytics, or cloud sync. You can forget individual wins or reset your entire local world above. Clearing browser data also clears your Mori.

    What if I’m away for a while?+

    Life happens. Your bond doesn’t decay, there are no daily streaks, and Mori won’t be upset. A running timer catches up with the clock when you return, then asks whether you finished. It never saves a win on its own.
