    What works now. What needs connecting.

    Can I buy the little device?+

    Not yet. The hardware is a detailed design concept, not a manufactured product. There’s nothing to buy or reserve here. The web console can talk to real models after the owner enables AI access; the little local world works right away.

    Is Mori using AI or listening to me?+

    Only when you ask. Send uses the selected provider; Talk starts one turn of browser speech recognition, then sends your words. No microphone starts on page load. Your browser’s speech service may process audio off-device. Read-aloud uses browser speech synthesis. This is simple turn-taking, not ChatGPT’s or Claude’s native realtime voice. Typing works in browsers without voice support.

    Does my ChatGPT or Claude subscription connect?+

    No. Mori uses OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google model APIs, with separate API access and billing configured by the owner. App subscriptions and past chats don’t transfer. Open Connections to check setup or unlock access with the owner’s code. No provider keys are entered in this page.

    What does Mori remember?+

    AI chats are separate for each provider and held only in this page’s memory, not saved on reload. Sending includes that provider’s conversation through the Mori server, and through Vercel AI Gateway if the owner enables that route. This app does not store server transcripts; providers and Gateway may process or retain data under their own policies. Your companion name, shell, bond, local focus session, and up to 24 wins stay in browser storage, as does your theme. Local focus task text is not sent to AI. New conversation clears only the selected chat; Reset my local world clears companion data, not your theme.

    What can the simple buttons do?+

    Ask, write, summarise, plan, translate, and explain text with your chosen model. Examples fill an editable draft; nothing is sent until you choose Send or finish a Talk turn. This first version supports three model providers, not every agent. It doesn’t access your email, calendar, files, or other accounts, and it can’t perform actions in them.

    Checking this Mori’s AI setup…

      The owner needs to enable AI access and add a provider on the server. There’s no key to paste here.

      Setting up your own Mori?

      Follow docs/AI_CONNECTIONS.md in the project. Set an owner access code, then explicitly enable an eligible Gateway route on your linked Vercel project or add a direct provider key. Refresh connections after setup. A configured route still needs a real request to verify it.

      This unlocks AI requests, not your ChatGPT or Claude account.

      About access, voice & privacy

      Mori uses provider APIs, with separate access and billing. ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini app subscriptions and chat history don’t automatically connect. This version does not connect other accounts or tools.

      Prompts and the selected AI’s history go through the Mori server to that provider, via Vercel AI Gateway when the owner enables that route. Chats are held in page memory, not saved by this app; providers and Gateway may process or retain data under their policies. Access uses a server-set HttpOnly session cookie. The code isn’t stored in browser storage.

      Talk starts only when you press it. Browser speech recognition may send audio to the browser’s service; read-aloud uses browser speech synthesis. This is turn-by-turn dictation and playback, not native realtime voice. Stop ends listening, playback, and waiting here, but an in-flight request may already have reached the provider.
